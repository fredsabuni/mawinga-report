Strategic recommendations

How to move forward

Each recommendation is a distinct priority — not a wishlist, but a sequence. The first one is the most important and applies to everything that follows.

PRIORITY 01

Product engineering process — requirements must come from customers

No feature should be built based on assumptions. Every item on the roadmap must be validated with dealers before development starts — through walkthroughs, co-design sessions, and observation in their actual shops.

Requirements come from customer interviews and real usage — not internal brainstorms.
Designs and prototypes are walked through with customers before any code is written.
No feature ships without a customer validation step.
Create a lightweight feedback loop so active users can flag pain points continuously.
PRIORITY 02

Radically simplify the UI

The platform is too complicated for non-technical dealers. Each role should land on a screen showing only their options. Design so clear that a first-time dealer needs no training.

Role-specific home screenMax 3 taps to any actionReduce navigation complexity
PRIORITY 03

Bulk IMEI entry via camera scan

Owners receive dozens of phones at a time. Typing each IMEI is painful and error-prone. Camera-based OCR from shipment labels would dramatically speed up stock entry and reduce mistakes.

OCR barcode scanConfirm before importError flagging
PRIORITY 04 — STANDALONE

Reports as a first-class feature

Reports are currently buried. They should be a prominent, dedicated section — showing who sold what, where, when. Stock aging surfaces inline. Business owners make daily decisions from this data.

Sales by agent / branchStock aging dashboardExpense vs income
PRIORITY 05

Agent commissions & payment integration

Dealers currently calculate agent commissions manually — tracking who sold what, calculating the amount owed, and making payments outside the system entirely. Mawinga should close this loop: once a sale is recorded, the commission is calculated automatically, and the owner can approve and disburse payment directly from the platform.

🧮
Auto-calculate commissions
When a sale is recorded, the agent's commission is calculated instantly based on configurable rates per model or flat percentage.
✅
Owner approval flow
Owner sees a pending commissions list — reviews each agent's sales, then approves individual or batch payments before anything is disbursed.
📲
Payment integration
Disburse via mobile money (M-Pesa, Airtel Money) directly from the approval screen. Agent receives payment and a notification confirming the amount.
Agent view: Agents can see their own commission balance and payment history — no more chasing the owner to know what they're owed.
Validate first: Commission structures vary by dealer. Walk through the approval flow and rate configuration with the customer before building anything.
PRIORITY 06 — FUTURE LAYER

Notifications & intelligent automation

Once core flows are stable and validated, the next layer of value is automation — the platform should proactively tell dealers what's happening and suggest actions based on their business patterns. This turns Mawinga from a recording tool into a business intelligence partner.

📦
Stock alerts
"Samsung A35 has been unsold for 35 days — consider returning or repricing."
📈
Performance nudges
"This branch sold significantly more last month. What's working there?"
🤖
Predictive modules
Auto-suggest reorder quantities based on historical sales velocity per model.
Important: Notifications and automation should only be enabled after core flows are stable and validated with customers. Automation on top of a broken UX adds noise, not value.

How we build next

The product engineering procedure

Every feature from here goes through this process — without exception. It protects the team's time and ensures we only build things dealers actually use.

1

Customer discovery

Interview active dealers. Find pain points in real daily work — not features they ask for by name, but the problems underneath.

2

Requirement definition

Write a clear problem statement and success criteria. What does "solved" look like for the dealer? How will we know it's working?

3

Design & walkthrough

Create a design (even rough wireframes like those above) and walk through with dealers. Capture feedback before a single line of code is written.

4

Build in small slices

Build the smallest version that can be tested. Don't build everything at once. A working prototype beats a perfect spec every time.

5

Test with real users

Put the feature in front of real dealers in their shop during real work. Watch where they get stuck. Don't explain it — see if it explains itself.

6

Iterate and release

Apply feedback, then ship. Measure adoption. If dealers aren't using it within 30 days, find out why before building the next thing.

There is a real opportunity here. Dealers want to go digital — they are already paying for tools. They just need a tool simple enough to actually use every day. Mawinga can be that tool, but only if we design with dealers, not just for them.
